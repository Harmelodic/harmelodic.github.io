# Feedback and Appreciation

- Feedback culture is good.
- Positive feedback (appreciation) is often more effective, but negative/constructive feedback is sometimes required.
- Showing appreciation is not done enough.
- Making space and time for feedback to show appreciation and learn for self-improvement helps people feel safe,
  listened to and improves the workplace.
- Formal feedback is good, but informal feedback (especially appreciation) is even better.
- Showing appreciation in the "little ways" is _hugely_ appreciated and impactful
	- e.g. I listened/watched/read/took to that recommendation you gave and I liked it!
- Consider ["Radical Candour"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical_Candor) - though sometimes rude or unkind criticism
  is excused as radical candour - so... do radical candour in a safe environment with people who know what radical
  candour actually is.

All comes back to basically: People like working with (genuinely) nice people.
