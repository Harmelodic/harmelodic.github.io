# Trust

Trust is not earned. Trust is given.

- Reflection of power structures
- Expectations but no trust is poor management & leadership
- Often entwined with micromanagement
- Often entwined with egos and self-importance
- Think of [John Cutler's doodle](https://bsky.app/profile/johncutle.fish/post/3lwksijde222y)

Everything is easier when the ego is put aside, trust is placed, and acceptance of failure is established.

If acceptance of failure is too risky, then place trust but provide plenty of support and good leadership & management
to increase the likelihood of success.

This is all related to the social-related parts of engineering.

For security purposes, zero-trust is needed, but that is more on the technical (IT, platform, architecture) side of
things.
