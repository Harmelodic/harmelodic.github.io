# Smartwatch wishes

## Watches and Smartwatches

Watches are great because they have very long battery lives (several years or more) and do what they need to: tell you
the time. Some watches can even have extra features, without really sacrificing battery life, like: a stopwatch, an
alarm, beep on the hour, tell you the date, moon phases.

A smartwatch is one that does even more (gather biometric data, give notifications, track GPS location, pay with
contactless, etc.) at the cost of:

- A more expensive watch - which is not great, but acceptable given the amount of functionality provided.
- A substantially shorter battery life (several days, maybe a month) - which is understandable, though it might not be
  acceptable for some (wasn't for me for a long time!), but acceptable for many, given the amount of functionality
  offered, and that recharging a device is not too inconvenient.
- Giving all the data gathered from the watch to the company who sold you the watch - which just _**shouldn't**_ really
  be a thing, but we accept because... that's how the world works now?

Let's wish for a better smartwatch.

### Wishes for a better smartwatch

The smartwatch should be able to connect to a "companion app" on a smartphone, and can upload and download information
from the companion app.

Smartwatch requirements:

- Has a long battery life (lasts for years, ideally; months is good; 1 month is OK; several days is unfortunate).
- Tells the time and date.
- Tells the weather (now and short-term future (up to 7 days?))
- Can make a sound and/or vibration at certain times (e.g. an alarm, or on-the-hour signal).
- Tells other time-related information (moon phases, tides, sunrise, sunset, etc.)
- Can act as a stopwatch and timer.
- Gather metrics for different data points:
    - heart rate
    - heart rate rhythm / variability
    - step count
    - GPS location
    - altitude / depth
    - speed / pace
    - temperature (body)
    - temperature (air/surroundings)
    - compass direction
    - barometer (air pressure)
    - blood-oxygen levels
    - etc.
- Can track "activities" (runs, swims, workouts, etc.)
- Gives basic tracking / graphed / mapped visualisations for each data point and activities.
- Can stream locally stored music or FM radio to headphones via secure local connection (e.g. bluetooth), assuming it
  does not significantly affect battery life (and if it does, then warn the user)
- Can have different watch faces that offer different amounts or kinds of information.
- Can share / backup saved settings (alarms, watch faces, etc.) and metrics to the companion app.
- Can receive phone notifications (provided by the companion app).
- Is repairable (can replace battery, screen, buttons, strap, etc.)
- Uses an open source operating system and firmware.

Companion app requirements:

- More in-depth visualisations and insights into different data points.
- Download firmware updates for connected smartwatches.
- Can configure which notifications are sent to the smartwatch.
- Provides data to the smartwatch that the smartwatch cannot gather itself (e.g. weather data, moon phases, etc.)
- Is open source and free.

Security & Privacy requirements:

- The smartwatch does not connect to the internet directly.
- The smartwatch and companion app communicate frequently (but not so much that ruins battery life) via a secure local
  connection (e.g. bluetooth)
- Connecting a smartwatch to a companion app does not automatically share any metrics. Sharing metrics must be "opt-in"
  and easy to "opt-out" of sharing any and all data points at any time.
- All metrics must stay on the smartwatch, unless explicitly shared (per data point) with the companion app.
- All metrics shared to the companion app must be stored securely and be inaccessible to other apps unless access is
  explicitly granted (and access can be removed at any time).
- All metrics available in the companion app should not be automatically shared to anywhere unless explicitly configured
  by the user to do so (e.g. automatically share runs on Strava)
- All automatic sharing of metrics can easily be limited to specific data points, or stopped completely.
- All metric data in the companion app or smartwatch should be exportable to a convenient file format (e.g. CSV) if the
  user wants the raw data.
- All metric data in the companion app should be exportable to an encrypted backup file for backing up.

Needless features:

- The smartwatch does not need perform contactless payments, a phone can do that and has better security (e.g.
  requiring fingerprint before payment)
- The smartwatch does not need to send or receive messages, a phone can do that with a much better user experience.
- The smartwatch does not need to take calls, a phone can do that with a much better user experience (and would ruin
  smartwatch battery life)
