# Equation Reference

When buying things, or doing research, I sometimes need to convert or calculate things. This involves equations that I
can never seem to remember, but I was taught in school and often can easily look up. Since the internet is becoming less
reliable, I'm just going to make a list of (forever-a-work-in-progress and non-exhaustive) equations that I can
reference here.

## Electricity

> Power = Voltage x Current  
> P = V x I

- Power in Watts (W).
- Voltage in Volts (V).
- Current in Amps (A).

---

> Voltage = Current x Resistance  
> V = I x R
>
> Voltage = Current x Impedance  
> V = I x Z

(a.k.a. Ohm's Law)

- Voltage in Volts (V).
- Current in Amps (A).
- Resistence in Ohms (&#937;).
- Impedance in Ohms (&#937;).

---

> Power = Current&sup2; x Resistance  
> P = I&sup2; x R

Basically, substitution of the above to 2 equations to remove calculate Power solely on Current and Resistance.

- Power in Watts (W).
- Current in Amps (A).
- Resistence in Ohms (&#937;).
