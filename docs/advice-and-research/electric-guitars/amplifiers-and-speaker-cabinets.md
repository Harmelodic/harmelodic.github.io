# Amplifiers and Speaker Cabinets

Often shortened to "Amps" and "Cabs".

An amp might just be its own device (sometimes called a "head"), and it then connects to 1 or more speaker cabinets that
will produce the sound. "Combo amps" are devices that contain both the amp and speakers in one cabinet-like device -
also (unhelpfully) referred to as simply "amps".

From here on out, I'll refer to things as amplifiers, speaker cabinets and combos.

I'll cover the basics of what the devices do below, but to understand how amplifiers and speaker cabinets affect tone, I

## Amplifiers

In their most basic form, amplifiers take a signal and amplify it.

Typically, amplifiers can come in three types:

- Tube amplifiers use vacuum tubes containing electrodes to analogue-ly amplify the guitar signal. This typically
  produces a "full" (or "warm") tones.
- Solid-state amplifiers convert the analog signal into a digital one, amplify using electronic circuitry, and convert
  the signal back into an analog signal for speakers.
- "Valvestate" amplifiers mainly use solid-state amplification, with an added tube before the solid-state amplification
  circuitry.

Most amplifiers also apply extra effects, like basic EQ control (bass, middle, treble) and gain controls, which will
also affect the tone.

The materials used & build quality is important for durability, especially if you're travelling with an amplifier.

### Gain vs Volume

Both measure and affect _loudness_ - measured in decibels (dB) - but in slightly different ways.

- Volume: How loud the output sound is. Increasing volume on will usually simply increase actual perceived volume. This
  is fed to speakers, where speakers will eventually either break or cause distortion when too loud.

- Gain: How loud the input sound is. Increasing gain will increase volume but will also likely increase distortion as a
  loud signal is fed into components and is "clipped".

## Speaker Cabinets

Speaker cabinets are boxes (or "cabinets") that contain speakers.

They can be bought in different forms that affect tone:

- Open-backed, close-backed, or partially open.
- Be large or small.
- They can contain speakers of different types and sizes.
- They can have different grills / coverings (which also protect the speakers).

The materials used & build quality is important for durability, especially if you're travelling with a speaker cabinet.

## Combos

Combos combine amplifiers and speaker cabinets into one device.

This can make buying an amplifier-plus-speaker-cabinet setup more affordable (depending on the combo you buy) and/or
more
convenient (only one device to haul around).
