# Electric Guitars Overview

Electric guitars make nice noises in different ways and there's a lot of equipment and factors to take into account,
especially when attempting to play with a specific "tone".

Typically, noise is made by an electric guitar through:

- [The Electric Guitar](./the-electric-guitar.md) producing the raw sound.
- Various pedals on a [pedalboard](pedals-and-pedalboards.md) modifying this sound with effects.
- The sound is fed into an [amplifier & cabinet](amplifiers-and-speaker-cabinets.md) speaker for amplification.
- (On a big stage, the cabinet speakers will likely be recorded by a microphone (e.g. SM-57), then fed into a mixing
  desk and then blasted out of big speakers for the audience to hear better.)

## Tone

Tone is the noun used to collectively describe the qualities of the guitar sound that you hear, other than the actual
note (pitch) of the note. Tone of the signal can be:

- Clean or Dirty/Boosted/Fuzzy/Overdriven/Distorted/Clipped
- Bright or Dark
- Warm or... not.
- Full or Thin
- Sustained (how long the note plays for after plucking).
- Modulated (changed) in different ways.

Many things affect tone - see the other pages for the specific parts to see how they affect tone.
