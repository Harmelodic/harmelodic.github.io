# Learning Languages

Mostly focused on learning grammar and Swedish.

## Conjugating verbs

Verbs change (or conjugate) according to tense (i.e. time) and subject / point-of-view (i.e. 1st person, 2nd person, 3rd
person).

I consistently find this confusing and struggle to remember, so this table helps:

|                            | I                         | We                         | You (singular or plural)    | He / She / One                | They (singular or plural)    |
|----------------------------|---------------------------|----------------------------|-----------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Past Simple                | I walked.                 | We walked.                 | You walked.                 | (S)he walked.                 | They walked.                 |
| Past Continuous            | I was walking.            | We were walking.           | You were walking.           | (S)he was walking.            | They were walking.           |
| Past Perfect Simple        | I had walked.             | We had walked.             | You had walked.             | (S)he had walked.             | They had walked.             |
| Past Perfect Continuous    | I had been walking.       | We had been walking.       | You had been walking.       | (S)he had been walking.       | They had been walking.       |
| Present Perfect Simple     | I have walked.            | We have walked.            | You have walked.            | (S)he has walked.             | They have walked.            |
| Present Perfect Continuous | I have been walking.      | We have been walking.      | You have been walking.      | (S)he has been walking.       | They have been walking.      |
| Present Simple             | I walk.                   | We walk.                   | You walk.                   | (S)he walks.                  | They walk.                   |
| Present Continuous         | I am walking.             | We are walking.            | You are walking.            | (S)he is walking.             | They are walking.            |
| Future Simple              | I will walk.              | We will walk.              | You will walk.              | (S)he will walk.              | They will walk.              |
| Future Continuous          | I will be walking.        | We will be walking.        | You will be walking.        | (S)he will be walking.        | They will be walking.        |
| Future Perfect Simple      | I will have walked.       | We will have walked.       | You will have walked.       | (S)he will have walked.       | They will have walked.       |
| Future Perfect Continuous  | I will have been walking. | We will have been walking. | You will have been walking. | (S)he will have been walking. | They will have been walking. |

Note:

- Perfect means "Completed". Comes from the latin "perfectum" which means completed.
- "You singular or plural" and "They singular or plural" may be different depending on the language.
- He, She and One might be result in different conjugations in other languages.
- Other languages may have more or less tenses, or have different words for different subjects (singular, plural,
  formal, etc.).
- Like with all grammar rules, there are exceptions and so not all verbs conjugate in the same way.
