# Interesting websites

There are some interesting websites on the internet. Here's some:

In Sweden, there is the MCF or "Myndigheten för civilt försvar" (Swedish Civil Defence and Resilience Agency) which used
to be called MSB or "Myndigheten för samhällsskydd och beredskap" (Swedish Civil Contingencies Agency). They have a map
of all the shelters in
Sweden: [🔗](https://msbgis.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/nearby/index.html?appid=8bfc89e7c6064bc181c6a1a6bbac2fa3)

Silk is a website I found years ago (back in 2013-ish?) where you can draw silky patterns with various symmetry and
colour options. The visual produced looks like it's made from silky light: [🔗](http://weavesilk.com)
